Project objective

Demonstrate an end-to-end, research-only chromosome 21 short-read NGS workflow: public data acquisition, read quality control, alignment, alignment QC, variant calling, filtering, normalization, functional annotation and a review-ready spreadsheet. This is a technical portfolio demonstration, not a diagnostic interpretation.

Workflow and outputs

StageMethod / result
InputENA ERR297183 paired-end FASTQ; 10,769,817 read pairs, 74 bp reads. Download integrity checked using ENA MD5 values.
Read QCFastQC and fastp. fastp improved Q30 from 77.07% to 94.85%, but retained only 7,704,083 pairs (about 28.5% read loss); original reads were used for primary alignment.
Reference and alignmentGRCh38 chromosome 21 reference; BWA-MEM; coordinate-sorted BAM produced and samtools quickcheck passed. Full-genome indexing attempt did not complete, so chromosome-only mapping is a material limitation.
Alignment QC1,221,496 mapped alignment records (5.67% of 21,545,691 total records); 566,606 alignments with MAPQ ≥30 after excluding unmapped and secondary records. chr21 mean depth 1.57×; 20.13% of bases covered ≥1×, 3.45% ≥10×, 1.36% ≥20× and 0.67% ≥30×.
Variant callingbcftools mpileup + call: 89,962 raw VCF records (including reference/nonvariant sites, not all confirmed variants).
Filtering and normalizationQUAL ≥30, sum of INFO/DP4 ≥10, ALT-supporting DP4 reads ≥3; 10,837 records retained before bcftools normalization. Normalized multiallelic representation yielded 10,943 unique annotated variants.
AnnotationEnsembl VEP REST; 10,943 inputs and 10,943 returned results. Full JSON and TSV saved in results/annotation.
Portfolio workbookAll_Variants, Prioritized_Variants, Variant_Review and README; 638 candidates selected for manual review using consequence terms.

Interpretation and next steps

Important limitations. Chromosome-only alignment can mis-map reads originating elsewhere in the genome. Mean depth is low, most chr21 positions have zero coverage, and no independent variant validation was performed. The 638 selected records are review candidates only; do not interpret them as pathogenic, clinically confirmed or comprehensively detected variants. The DP4-derived ALT fraction is a read-support estimate, not a validated VAF.

Next: inspect candidate loci in IGV, assess mapping quality and strand/read support, check transcript-level consequences, and document external evidence separately. Any downstream interpretation should preserve the exploratory nature of these data.
